## What not to do right now
- Do not start multiple new products in parallel.
- Do not create a super-app spanning home services, government, health, education and business before one narrow use case is validated.
- Do not build a new standalone VISION app before proving a need for it.
- Do not merge ANVORA, Best Version, CHRONO VYU, LYKOSHA/LYNCOSA, Yuva On, or other names based only on similar themes.
- Do not invent missing definitions, market sizes, user interviews, revenue, or product-market fit.
- Do not promote a venture because it sounds like a unicorn or could be acquired.
- Do not change CAT schedule, learning sequence, streaks, or saved progress as collateral maintenance.
- Do not claim automatic background work, reminders, builds, commits or deployments unless the relevant tool actually performed and verified them.

## Weekly review — manager output
For each active item, report only:
1. Status: On track / Blocked / Needs evidence / Parked.
2. Evidence added this week.
3. Next smallest action.
4. Time/cost risk.
5. Continue / Pause / Pivot / Archive recommendation.

Keep no more than **one major build** active during the CAT-first period. Research notes and small learning activities must remain time-boxed and subordinate to CAT.

## Naming and source-recovery rule
The portfolio contains variants and possibly overlapping ideas. Maintain aliases and original source text until the definitions can be compared. If authoritative source material is missing, mark **SOURCE NEEDED** rather than guessing. New items are classified as **Core Feature**, **Future Parking**, or **Research Idea**, preserving the existing master structure.
